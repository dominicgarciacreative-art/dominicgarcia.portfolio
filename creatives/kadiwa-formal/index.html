Kadiwa Formal — event branding

Kadiwa Formal

A formal concert night for the Locale of Novaliches. One key visual, blue curtain, light leaks and a silver title, carried across every touchpoint of the event.

01 · Key visual & posters

The poster set the whole look. Built at print size, with and without the registration contacts.

02 · Countdown stories

Six Instagram Stories counting down to the night, one a day, so the date stayed in the feed all week.

Instagram Stories · 1080 × 1920 · 6 days to 1

03 · Screens

The same key visual opened the night on the venue screen, at 1920 × 1080.

Concert banner · 1920 × 1080

04 · Motion graphics

Everything that moved: a 3D intro and logo reveal, the 1 Day To Go motion story, the Kadiwa lyric video, the full-song video and the same-day edit cut on the night.

Short pieces loop like GIFs · longer videos open in full with the play button

05 · House rules video

The final 60-second house rules video, cut to the voiceover from the ten-scene storyboard and played before the program.

Watch full video ↗

House rules video · 1920 × 1080 · 58 seconds · final render

06 · Event materials

Wristband, food stub and completion card, printed in the same curtain-and-light system so guests carried the brand through the night.
